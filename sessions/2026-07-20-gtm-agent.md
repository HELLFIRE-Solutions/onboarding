## Phase 1 — Context and why (done)

gtm-agent = module 1 in build priority: qualifying warm leads (LinkedIn/referrals, explicitly not mass cold email — legal risk in DACH), personalized outreach, calendar booking. Chosen first because it both sells and demos itself.

## Phase 2 — Architecture decisions (done)

Three decisions from `docs/architecture.md` worth being able to explain without looking them up:
1. **Orchestration** — a custom lightweight orchestrator on the Anthropic SDK, not LangGraph. Reason: Etap 1's pipeline is linear (ingest → qualify → draft → [human approval] → send → track → log); LangGraph is only justified once real branching shows up (Etap 2, per-client ICP logic).
2. **CRM = HubSpot Free** — zero cost at the current lead volume, has a built-in Meetings tool (covers booking without a second integration), integration behind a narrow interface (`integrations/hubspot.py`) so Etap 2 can swap CRMs.
3. **Legitimate interest gate** — a `LegitimateInterestRecord` is required for every lead, checked **in code** (`MissingLegitimateInterestError`), not just a process requirement.

## Phase 5 — Guardrails and limits (done)

- Without an active `LegitimateInterestRecord` (or with `opt_out_at` set) — the agent refuses to draft. Verified by a test, not just documentation.
- No message goes out without an explicit human `approve` (`status: draft → approved → sent`, `send` only takes `approved`).
- Explicit non-goal: no LinkedIn scraping or auto-messaging — leads are added to the CSV manually.
